MEASUREMENT IS ACTIVE ON DEPLOYMENT

Measure visits. Follow curiosity.

PostHog captures page views, links and interactions automatically, and records masked sessions. A Formspree submission counts as a lead only after successful delivery. Outreach posts and channel performance will be documented from real campaign evidence.

Campaign links carry utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign. Compare visitors and confirmed signups using the same channel and reporting window; retain the actual post links and dates rather than estimates.

PROOF TO ADD

Outreach links & screenshots

For every post, attach the permalink, publication date, anonymized screenshot, UTM link, and any moderation approval. A post isn't counted until it actually appears.

PROOF TO ADD

Measurement screenshots

Add dated PostHog web analytics and funnel screenshots and an aggregate Formspree submission count. Never display subscriber email addresses publicly.

03 / HOW WE WILL DECIDE

Learn from behavior.
Not applause.

What counts as traction?

Visitors arriving from a repeatable channel, meaningful prototype clicks, and consented early-access registrations. We will not infer paying demand from a waitlist alone.

What if a channel underperforms?

Compare visits, clicks and confirmed signups per channel. Record distribution effort and audience fit before choosing where to focus the next test.

Campaign status: Formspree and PostHog are configured, with session recording enabled in PostHog. Publishing campaign evidence and comparing channels requires real distribution and real visitor data.
