FROM A HEADLINE TO ITS EVIDENCE

Stay curious.
Keep the final say.

This is the experience we are exploring. Start with an article or a shared claim. Check when it matters to you.

01 / CHOOSE

Something needs a second look.

Ask LogiQ to check an article, or bring a link or text. In the prototype, choose one of four news scenarios.

02 / UNDERSTAND

See why, in plain language.

Review individual claims and see what the available evidence supports, contradicts, or leaves unresolved.

03 / QUESTION

Follow the evidence.

Inspect an original source, understand missing context, copy a summary, or return to reading.

A SECOND LOOK, NOT THE LAST WORD

Clear evidence.
Room for uncertainty.

LogiQ is meant to support your reasoning, not replace it. The distinction matters.

CLAIMS & EVIDENCE

Look at each claim.

One article can contain both supported and misleading statements. The prototype shows claim-level assessments against curated sources.

CONTEXT, NOT CERTAINTY

Leave room for nuance.

Missing context and insufficient evidence are distinct outcomes. Neither emotional language nor missing evidence alone proves a claim false.

YOUR PACE, YOUR CHOICE

Close it and keep reading.

Source details, sharing, and feedback are optional. This preview sends no input or feedback data and runs no live analysis.
