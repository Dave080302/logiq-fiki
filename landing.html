FROM A HEADLINE TO ITS EVIDENCE

Stay curious.
Keep the final say.

This is the experience we are exploring. Keep reading an article or browsing your feed. Open the LogiQ alert whenever you want a closer look.

01 / READ

An alert, right when it matters.

LogiQ reviews content automatically and shows a small LogiQ notification when something needs a second look. The prototype simulates this with three sample stories.

02 / UNDERSTAND

See why, in plain language.

Tap the alert to expand findings and sources together. See what the evidence supports, contradicts, or leaves unresolved.

03 / QUESTION

Follow the evidence.

Inspect an original source, understand missing context, or return to reading.

A SECOND LOOK, NOT THE LAST WORD

Clear evidence.
Room for uncertainty.

LogiQ is meant to support your reasoning, not replace it. The distinction matters.

CLAIMS & EVIDENCE

Look at each claim.

One article can contain both supported and misleading statements. The prototype shows claim-level assessments against curated sources.

CONTEXT, NOT CERTAINTY

Leave room for nuance.

Missing context and insufficient evidence are distinct outcomes. Neither emotional language nor missing evidence alone proves a claim false.

YOUR PACE, YOUR CHOICE

Close it and keep reading.

Dismiss the alert or close the review at any time. Sources expand inline; the same flow works on desktop and mobile.
